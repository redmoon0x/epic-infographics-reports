Research Methodology (v2)

  • Tooling change from v1: The first version of this report relied on a generic web-extraction tool that Naukri.com and Indeed actively block (CAPTCHA/406 errors), forcing reliance on search-engine snippets for many listings. This revision used a real, full browser session (Aether MCP) that successfully loads and renders live Naukri and LinkedIn pages, allowing every listing below to be opened and read in full rather than inferred from a snippet.
  • Verification process: Every "Verified" job in this report was opened at its live URL, its full job description read end-to-end, and its experience requirement checked against her actual 0-year/fresher-with-internship profile. Several postings that looked fresher-friendly from the title or search snippet (Amazon, HireGenie, E2E Rail, Creative Hands HR) were opened, found to be mismatched or non-live, and explicitly excluded rather than included with a caveat.
  • Company legitimacy: Checked via AmbitionBox and Glassdoor rating pages (job security, salary, and overall satisfaction scores quoted where available), plus company-website/LinkedIn cross-checks for smaller firms (Careernet, Forward Impact, Ricaptos, Ordrio, the two CA firms).
  • Geography discipline: Listings outside the requested priority order (Bengaluru, Remote, Udupi, Mangaluru) — e.g. Hyderabad-only postings — were excluded even when otherwise well-matched, to keep the shortlist focused.
  • Recruiter emails: Only included where independently published on the company's own site or the job posting itself. Where none exists (most large-company ATS-based postings), the report says so explicitly rather than guessing.
  • What changed from v1: Discover Dollar Technologies is fully removed. Several previously "Verified Active" v1 listings (Zentegra, Textron, Amentum, Ennismore/Hoxton, Infosys BPM walk-in, Evalme) were superseded in this pass by newly verified alternatives; they may still be worth checking manually if still live, but are not repeated here to keep this report focused on what was freshly re-verified in this session.
