Research Methodology

  • Sources used: LinkedIn Jobs (primary — most reliably extractable), Naukri.com search snippets, Indeed India, Foundit, Wellfound, official company career pages (Discover Dollar, Fivetran, LaunchDarkly, S&P Global, ICRA Analytics, Genpact), AmbitionBox and Glassdoor for company reputation, and Justdial/Facebook/company websites for local Mangaluru/Udupi firm verification.
  • Verification process: Every job listed as "Verified" was opened at its actual posting URL (not just a search-engine snippet) to confirm title, location, experience requirement, and content. Listings that could only be found via search snippets — because Naukri.com and Indeed blocked direct automated access with CAPTCHA/bot-detection during this session — are explicitly marked "Could Not Fully Verify" rather than presented as confirmed.
  • Freshness: Where LinkedIn displayed a relative post age (e.g. "15 hours ago," "1 week ago"), it is reported as seen at retrieval time (17 Sept 2026). Postings older than ~2 months, or showing signs of being stale/recycled, were flagged or excluded from Tier A.
  • Company legitimacy: Checked via presence of an official company domain, LinkedIn company page, and independent review platforms (Glassdoor/AmbitionBox). Local Mangaluru/Udupi firms were additionally cross-checked against Justdial and Facebook business listings.
  • Salary sourcing: Only salary figures explicitly stated on a job posting, or sourced from AmbitionBox's aggregated salary-report pages, are shown. Market-average figures are labelled as estimates, never presented as the advertised salary for a specific role.
  • Recruiter emails: Only included where publicly published on the job posting or the firm's own contact page (Shetty & Bhat, Anantha & Co). Where a recruiter's name was published but no direct email was found (Equiniti, RAQMEA, Discover Dollar internal HR), the report says so explicitly and recommends LinkedIn or the official portal instead.
  • Honest limitation: Naukri.com and Indeed.com — India's largest job boards by volume — actively blocked automated browsing throughout this research (406/CAPTCHA on Naukri, Cloudflare block on Indeed). This means true market volume for fresher finance/AP roles in Bengaluru is very likely higher than what could be independently verified here.
