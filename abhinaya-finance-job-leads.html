04 — Methodology

How each lead was verified

Research was run through parallel deep-research agents using the Aether browser MCP (live browser sessions that bypass Naukri/LinkedIn bot-blocking that plain HTTP fetch tools hit) plus targeted web/Bing dorks. Every listing in this report was individually opened and read — not inferred from a search snippet. For the "Posted Today" pass specifically, LinkedIn was queried with its own f_TPR=r86400 (past 24 hours) date filter combined with f_E=1,2 (entry-level) and sorted newest-first, then each candidate listing's own job-detail page was opened to read its exact "X minutes/hours ago" timestamp as ground truth — search-result-list timestamps were not trusted alone. Naukri's "Last 1 Day" freshness filter was used the same way. Company legitimacy was cross-checked against AmbitionBox/Glassdoor ratings and, for suspicious remote listings, against known scam-network reporting. Postings that could not be independently verified as currently active, that turned out to be mislabeled/wrong-location/expired on direct inspection, or that traced to documented scam operators were excluded rather than padded in — including three "Financial Analyst Internship" postings linked to the Unified Mentor scam network, found and excluded during the 18 September pass.
