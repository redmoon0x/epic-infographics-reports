Methodology

Research was run in two passes using live browser automation (not cached search snippets):

  1. Discovery pass — two parallel research agents covered Bangalore and Udupi/Mangalore separately, searching LinkedIn Jobs (via its public jobs-guest detail API, which bypasses the authwall for individual postings) and Naukri.com directly, plus Bing-based searches for LinkedIn recruiter posts with inline contact emails.
  2. Verification pass — every single lead surfaced in the discovery pass was re-opened individually to confirm the exact posting date/recency text, the full stated experience requirement, any hidden disqualifying requirement buried in the JD body (specific degree, certification, years-of-experience), and to search separately for a recruiter/HR email.

Naukri posting dates were additionally cross-checked against the DDMMYY date code embedded in each job's URL. All 15 jobs in this report were confirmed live and dated on or after 5 September 2026 as of the verification date, 20 September 2026.

No recruiter emails were fabricated. Where no email could be found, the report says so explicitly and directs to the platform's own Apply button, or — where available — a named recruiter's LinkedIn profile or a direct phone/WhatsApp contact.
