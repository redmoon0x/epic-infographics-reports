04 — Do this before paying

Checkout checklist

1. Confirm the exact processorIt should say Ryzen 3 7320U for the Dell or Acer. Do not accidentally buy a similarly named Celeron model.
2. Confirm storageThe Acer has both 256GB and 512GB listings. The Dell should show 512GB SSD.
3. Confirm WindowsThe product title must say Windows 11 Home. Avoid DOS or “no operating system” variants.
4. Check seller and warrantyReview seller rating, brand warranty, replacement window and delivery inspection terms.
5. Check the final cart pricePrices and bank offers change by location, account, stock and payment method.
6. Test immediately after deliveryCheck the screen, webcam, microphone, Wi-Fi, keyboard, charger, SSD capacity and Windows activation during the replacement window.
Final answer: Dell Inspiron 3535 on Flipkart is the one I would buy. Amazon's Acer is acceptable only if the 512GB variant is below ₹35,000. Avoid the ASUS unless keeping the spend near ₹30,000 matters more than speed.
